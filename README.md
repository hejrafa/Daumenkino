# Daumenkino

**Große Filme. Kurze Meinung.**

Big films. Short takes.

Daumenkino is a German film podcast and a shared film journal by **Ann-Sophie and Rafael**. It comes from a love of going to the cinema, talking about what stays with us afterwards, and occasionally disagreeing about almost everything we just watched.

[Visit daumenkino.fm](https://daumenkino.fm/) · [Listen to the podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/de/podcast/daumenkino/id1476457786) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/daumenkino.fm/)

## The podcast

We have been talking films together on Daumenkino since **24 July 2019**, when our first episode, **“#000 Die Namensfindung,”** was released. What began as roughly hour-long conversations about upcoming films is getting a shorter format: **one to two minutes of audio, one or two sentences on the website**.

The idea stays the same: personal reactions, strong opinions, and a little film trivia. Something to take away from a film—or a reason to go and see it yourself.

The [podcast archive](https://podcasts.apple.com/de/podcast/daumenkino/id1476457786) is still there to listen to. The first episode’s release date is recorded in our [official podcast feed](https://anchor.fm/s/cbef5f8/podcast/rss).

## The website

[daumenkino.fm](https://daumenkino.fm/) is the visual home of Daumenkino: big film stills, bold typography, and short reviews straight from our Letterboxd accounts.

- **Two perspectives.** Browse both of our reviews together, or filter by Ann-Sophie or Rafael. Each card links to the full review on Letterboxd.
- **A little Daumenkino.** The name is German for *flipbook*. Hover over a review and a few stills from the film flick past behind the text, then return to the cover image.
- **Our favorites.** The footer shows the four favorite films from each of our Letterboxd profiles.
- **Share a take.** Every review card has a share button that turns it into a 1080×1920 image for Instagram Stories: the share sheet on phones, a download on desktops.
- **German and English.** Switch the website’s language at any time. Our original review wording stays as written.
- **Room to read.** Keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, and reduced-motion support help keep the site usable. Hover previews stay still on touch devices and when reduced motion is enabled.

We publish our reviews on Letterboxd, and the website picks up new reviews automatically, approximately **every six hours**. Film backdrops come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/); films without additional stills keep a static image.
